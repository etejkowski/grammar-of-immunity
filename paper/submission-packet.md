### Four things to confirm on their guide-for-authors page

That page blocks automated access, so these are unresolved. Paste the relevant
text to the assistant and every file gets conformed in one pass.

1. **Abstract limit.** Ours is 242 words, so a 250-word cap no longer bites —
   the earlier 264-word figure predates the abstract revisions. Only a cap of
   200 or below would force a cut, and the first thing to go is the TCRdist
   parenthetical "(0.6281 vs 0.5893)", since Table 5 reports it.
2. **Abstract headings.** Ours are Background / Methods / Results / Validation /
   Conclusions. "Validation" is non-standard; if they mandate a fixed set, it
   merges into Results.
3. **Highlights.** Required or optional, and their exact character limit.
4. **Reference style.** Ours is numbered, Vancouver-like. Also confirm whether
   they want figure captions in the manuscript or in a separate file.

### Also worth reading before you submit

Their generative-AI policy. The declaration is already in the manuscript, before
the references, and it states what the assistant did and what you verified. Know
how they interpret the policy going in rather than at acceptance.

## Pre-flight checks

```bash
python3 paper/check_highlights.py          # 85-char limit and count agreement
.venv/bin/codespell -L FPR paper/*.md README.md   # typos (-L FPR: false positive)
python3 paper/build.py                     # DOCX + HTML + PDF + plain abstract
```

`paper/manuscript.md` is the single source of truth. Do not hand-edit the DOCX —
that is what caused the V2 divergence, which dropped an ORCID and left a dangling
sentence in the Discussion.
