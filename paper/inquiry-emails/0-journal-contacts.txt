=========================================================================
FIVE THINGS TO CHECK ON WHICHEVER PAGE YOU OPEN
Paste the relevant text to the assistant and every file is conformed in one pass.
=========================================================================
1. Publishing model — hybrid, fully OA, or subscription. Models flip.
2. Page, colour-figure or supplementary charges even without OA.
3. Abstract limit, and whether the structured headings are a fixed set.
   Ours is 242 words with headings Background / Methods / Results /
   Validation / Conclusions. "Validation" is non-standard. A 250-word cap
   does not bite; only 200 or below forces a cut.
4. Whether Highlights are required, and their character limit. Five are
   ready, longest 78 characters.
5. Their generative-AI policy. The manuscript already carries a declaration
   before the references.
